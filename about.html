Aryan Sharma

Born into a family of Vedic scholars in Jaipur, India, Aryan Sharma began studying astrology at age 12 under the guidance of his grandfather — a celebrated Jyotishi of the Rajputana court lineage. After earning a Master's degree in Astronomy and completing a formal 7-year Gurukul apprenticeship, he moved to integrate Vedic precision with the psychological depth of Western astrology.

Today, AstroVeda serves clients across 40+ countries, from Fortune 500 executives seeking timing for strategic decisions, to individuals navigating life transitions. Every reading is prepared with full chart calculations, no AI automation — only human expertise.
